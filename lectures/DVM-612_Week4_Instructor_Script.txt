DVM 612 Week 4 instructor script
Instructor: Dr. Yujin Kim, D.V.M., Ph.D., FFCP

38 slides. Two real hospital cases: Willie (Cavalier, ASA III-E, sedated for ear clean) and MoMo (DSH, ASA IV-E, exploratory cancelled). Owner names, addresses, phones, and emails stay off slides and off this script.

SLIDE 1. LONG ISLAND UNIVERSITY  ·  LEWYT COLLEGE OF VETERINARY MEDICINE
    SPEAK: Welcome. This hour is preoperative evaluation, patient and surgeon preparation, and postoperative care. Two real patients from the same hospital. Willie, a 6-year 11-month MN Cavalier, 13.7 kg, acute vestibular crisis plus left otitis. We sedated him. MoMo, a 6-year SF DSH, 4.25 kg, vomiting that looked like a foreign-body surgery. Imaging cancelled the exploratory. Assign both ASA statuses. Owner identifiers stay off these slides. Thirty-eight slides. If discussion runs, protect antiseptics, both cases, and recovery.

SLIDE 2. DVM 612 WEEK 4  ·  38 SLIDES
    SPEAK: Read the five objectives aloud. Timer: protect Part II (prep pictures) and both cases.

SLIDE 3. WHY THIS HOUR EXISTS
    SPEAK: A closure that hemorrhages in recovery is a failed surgery. The first 24 hours are part of the operation.

SLIDE 4. EACH PRINCIPLE HAS A PREOPERATIVE OR POSTOPERATIVE ACTION
    SPEAK: Two minutes, then move. Every Halsted principle has a preop or postop action, not just an intraoperative one.

SLIDE 5. PREOPERATIVE, PREPARATION, INTRAOPERATIVE, POSTOPERATIVE
    SPEAK: Anesthesia prepares the patient for surgical prep. Surgery owns prep through the last skin suture. Both own recovery.

SLIDE 6. EXAMINE, REQUEST INDICATED LABS, INTERPRET, THEN ASSIGN ASA
    SPEAK: Four jobs. Students often skip #3 and #4. Do your own PE, then use technician vitals as a second set of numbers. Order: examine, then premedicate.

SLIDE 7. AMERICAN SOCIETY OF ANESTHESIOLOGISTS CLASSIFICATION
    SPEAK: Read Status 1, then 3, then 4 slowly. E means emergency. Next: four 60-second cases, then Willie (3-E, we sedated) and MoMo (4-E, exploratory cancelled).

SLIDE 8. ASSIGN STATUS, THEN SAY WHETHER YOU PROCEED TODAY
    SPEAK: Cold-call four students. C is Willie (sedation). D is MoMo (exploratory cancelled).

SLIDE 9. 6 Y 11 MO MN CAVALIER KING CHARLES SPANIEL, 13.7 KG, BCS 6/9
    SPEAK: Record the murmur. Right-sided knuckling with left circling: treat as central vestibular plus otitis, then sedate. Aminoglycosides are a risk if the middle ear is involved even when the drum looks present. Skip NSAID after DexSP.

SLIDE 10. 6 YO SF DSH, 4.25 KG, BCS 5/9
    SPEAK: MoMo is the cat whose films looked like maybe GI and were kidneys. Cold-call: who would have clipped her on history alone? Be respectful; this cat died. The teaching point is judgment.

SLIDE 11. TESTS INDICATED FOR THIS PATIENT AND THIS PROCEDURE
    SPEAK: Request labs that change the plan. They inform the ASA number. Today’s PE writes it.

SLIDE 12. THESE BANDS INFORM STATUS. TODAY’S PE WRITES IT.
    SPEAK: Ninety seconds. Point at PCV, platelets, creatinine, potassium. Willie: CBC essentially Status 1. His Status 3-E is the vestibular exam. MoMo: WBC ~26 and creatinine 3.0 then 4.71.

SLIDE 13. LABS INFORM ASA STATUS. THE PHYSICAL EXAMINATION ASSIGNS IT.
    SPEAK: Students want to average the columns. The worst compensated vital-system problem that is a constant threat sets the floor. MoMo’s kidneys set Status 4. Willie’s neuro exam set Status 3 even with a normal CBC.

SLIDE 14. WHEN TO DELAY, WHEN TO PROCEED URGENTLY
    SPEAK: Resuscitate first, then clip. Delay elective OHE for pyoderma. Take GDV to surgery after resuscitation. MoMo: image and run labs, then cancel the exploratory.

SLIDE 15. 2020 AAHA ANESTHESIA AND MONITORING GUIDELINES; THEN THE RISK TALK
    SPEAK: Cite Grubb et al., 2020 AAHA Anesthesia and Monitoring Guidelines: healthy adults, food 4 to 6 hours, water until premedication. Neonates and patients under 2 kg: food fast no longer than 1 to 2 hours. A 2-minute risk talk prevents a 2-hour complaint. Mention DNR.

SLIDE 16. WHO-ADAPTED TIMEOUT AND MULTIMODAL PLAN
    SPEAK: Checklist takes 90 seconds and prevents wrong-site and forgotten cefazolin. Locals are underused.

SLIDE 17. TEACHING FORM. COMPLETE THE HEADER BEFORE THE FIRST DRUG.
    SPEAK: Walk the header: ASA III-E is written before alfaxalone. DexSP means skip NSAID. Grid every 5 minutes while sedated. Recovery boxes are part of the same page.

SLIDE 18. PREOPERATIVE EVALUATION DOCUMENTED; EXPLORATORY CANCELLED
    SPEAK: The form is not only for patients who get clipped. Recording the cancellation is a surgical document. Say it once, respectfully, then continue.

SLIDE 19. FREY ET AL. 2022 AAFP/AAHA: WHEN. WHITTEM 1999 AND GONZALEZ 2017: HOW.
    SPEAK: Do not attribute 22 mg/kg every 90 minutes to AAHA 2022. That guideline says when: 30 to 60 minutes before incision, skip clean OHE, stop postop. Whittem 1999 timed a second dose if surgery lasted more than 90 minutes. Gonzalez 2017 used 22 mg/kg IV. Elective canine OHE: skip the 14-day cephalexin prescription.

SLIDE 20. PREP ROOM, THEN OPERATING ROOM
    SPEAK: Airway and plane before clippers. Veterinary Betadine labels: 7.5% scrub, lather about 5 minutes, rinse, paint 5% Solution Veterinary, allow to dry. Eye: Roberts 1986, 1:50 of 10% PVP-I stock, 2-minute scrub plus 2-minute soak. Do not paint 10% from the veterinary bottle; that bottle is 5%.

SLIDE 21. #40 CLIPPER AFTER INDUCTION. FIELD 20 CM BEYOND THE PLANNED INCISION.
    SPEAK: Left photo is still too narrow. Right is a real 24-hour OHE. Willie: TECA field is pinna and skull. MoMo: skip the clippers.

SLIDE 22. READ THE BOTTLE. CITE THE LABEL OR THE PAPER. DO NOT INVENT A FOSSUM PAGE.
    SPEAK: Write the bottle math on the board. Veterinary Betadine Solution is 5 percent, not 10 percent. Roberts 1986: 1 to 50 of 10 percent stock, 2-minute scrub plus 2-minute soak. One case of corneal edema at 1 to 2. Nolvasan: 2 percent CHG acetate, wash 2 to 4 minutes, keep out of eyes. Willie: swollen tympanum. Canal and periocular mucosa: detergent-free dilute PVP-I, not 7.5 percent scrub.

SLIDE 23. CENTER TO PERIPHERY. DIRTY PREP, THEN STERILE PREP.
    SPEAK: Mime the spiral. Clock about 5 minutes for veterinary Betadine scrub on intact skin. Recite Roberts 1 to 50 of 10 percent stock for the eye: 2 minutes plus 2 minutes. If the bottle is 5 percent veterinary solution, 1 to 25 matches 0.2 percent.

SLIDE 24. DORSAL RECUMBENCY, AIRWAY, IV CATHETER, MONITORING, V-TROUGH
    SPEAK: Airway and monitoring are on before the clip. Keep hips in a neutral spread.

SLIDE 25. FOUR-QUADRANT TOWELS, THEN THE LARGE DRAPE
    SPEAK: Left is a real spay with hair at the window. Re-clip or re-drape. Then timeout before you cut.

SLIDE 26. TECHNIQUE DIAGRAM AND OPERATING-ROOM PHOTOGRAPH
    SPEAK: Call out closed gloving. Students name SpO2, ETCO2, ECG, temp, fluids off the workstation photo.

SLIDE 27. RECOGNIZE, ANNOUNCE, CORRECT. THEN PROTECT THE PATIENT.
    SPEAK: Praise the person who says I just contaminated my sleeve. Then re-glove. Ureter injury is a calm, well-exposed patient problem.

SLIDE 28. WHAT DO YOU DO NEXT?
    SPEAK: Two minutes. B is Roberts 1986 bottle math. D is CHG off the cornea. C is the stabilize-first cat.

SLIDE 29. REMAIN WITH THE PATIENT UNTIL AIRWAY AND CIRCULATION ARE STABLE
    SPEAK: Real recovery: e-collar, IV, clipped abdomen. Pale OHE: stay at the cage, return to OR if unstable.

SLIDE 30. TEACHING FORM
    SPEAK: Students should be able to fill this after a spay. Photograph it.

SLIDE 31. SCORE PAIN. THEN TEACH THE E-COLLAR.
    SPEAK: Licking and unsupervised running are the two discharge failures after otherwise adequate closure.

SLIDE 32. HEMORRHAGE, AIRWAY, HERNIA, DEHISCENCE, SSI, SEROMA
    SPEAK: Hemorrhage versus seroma. Skin versus fascial dehiscence. Evisceration protocol in one breath.

SLIDE 33. WHAT THE OVERNIGHT CLINICIAN AND THE OWNER BOTH NEED
    SPEAK: At 2 a.m. someone opens this record because the abdomen is swelling. They need ligatures, sponge count, linea suture, and whether the client was called.

SLIDE 34. THE CLIENT MUST BE ABLE TO REPEAT THESE
    SPEAK: Pale gums + distended abdomen after OHE: come now. Return to OR.

SLIDE 35. ASA STATUS 3-E
    SPEAK: Walk Willie without questions until the end. Then one sentence on MoMo: the same hour of preoperative evaluation cancelled her exploratory.

SLIDE 36. BOTH RECEIVED A PREOPERATIVE EVALUATION. ONLY WILLIE WAS ANESTHETIZED.
    SPEAK: Last content slide if time is gone. Healthy Lab OHE is only the ASA I contrast.

SLIDE 37. Key points
    SPEAK: Stop here if time is gone. They should defend Willie III-E and MoMo IV-E without looking.

SLIDE 38. DVM 612  ·  PRINCIPLES OF SURGERY
    SPEAK: Take questions. If none: Willie versus MoMo ASA, then iodine: Roberts 1 to 50 of 10 percent stock, 2 minutes plus 2 minutes on the eye; intact skin is the 7.5 percent scrub for about 5 minutes then 5 percent veterinary paint. Dismiss on time.
