I am writing to ask whether you would join me as methods co-author and IRB co-investigator.

I have drafted a *Journal of Veterinary Medical Education* **Teaching Tip** that describes a six-gate scaffold I use to teach small animal emergency reasoning now that licensed formularies retrieve many milligram-per-kilogram numbers. The gates are species, localization, refusal of a silent corpus, outdated versus current source, the datum that would flip the plan, and consent when the disease is decompressible. Students write a six-line answer before they may open a monograph. That manuscript reports **no student data**. It should stay a Teaching Tip.

The paper I cannot write well alone is the **Educational Research Report**. JVME will want ethics approval, a pre-specified rubric, and blinded scoring. I am a clinician-educator. I need a colleague who designs learner studies, lives in IRBManager, and will stop me from treating a class hour as a p-value.

**What is already done**

- Teaching Tip draft, cover letter, and composite vignettes (no client identifiers; DVM 612’s 37 slides are not being rewritten for this).
- An IRB protocol skeleton aimed at Common Rule exempt categories 1 and/or 2, with the explicit note that **I cannot self-determine exemption**.
- A student information sheet and a 0–6 rubric (species, space, don’t, flip, sourced dose or △, consent if decompressible).

**What I am asking you to own**

- Methods: pairing of pre/post vignettes, rater blinding, agreement statistic, and the analysis lock *before* we see scripts.
- IRBManager submission with me; CITI Social-Behavioral-Educational + RCR. Hold the consent key if I am the course grader, or delay my access until grades are posted.
- Co-scoring a subset so I am not the only rater of my own students.
- Corresponding methods author on the research paper. I remain first author on the Teaching Tip and clinical content.

**What this is not**

- A product study of Instinct or any commercial tool. Students are not required to open one.
- Use of hospital charts or owner names.
- Extra work for you as a content expert in emergency medicine.

**Time.** One IRB packet, one 50-minute session in a course I already teach, scoring of one cohort’s short scripts, one manuscript. If the first cohort is small, we stop at a honest N rather than inventing a second fake sample.

If this is of interest, I can send the Teaching Tip and the IRB skeleton this week and book 30 minutes. If you would rather name someone else in assessment or the School of Education, I would be grateful for the introduction.

Thank you for considering it.
