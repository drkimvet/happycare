Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, “A Six-Gate Scaffold for Teaching Clinical Reasoning When Licensed Formularies Retrieve the Dose,” for publication as a **Teaching Tip**.

This is not an Educational Research Report. It does not contain learner outcome data, survey results, or a pilot study. The paper describes a dual-process teaching method for small animal emergency reasoning now that licensed formularies and retrieval-based tools supply many milligram-per-kilogram numbers. Students must complete six named gates before they may write a dose.

I confirm that the manuscript is original, is not under review elsewhere, and contains no client or student identifiers. No ethics board review was required because no human-subjects data are reported. I have no conflicts of interest and received no funding for this work.
