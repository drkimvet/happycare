# Five-Minute reading list (official Wiley only)

This is a **title list**, not a download. Do not fetch PDFs from third-party book mirrors. If the attending later supplies a purchased/library file, summarize it; never dump a chapter.

Neither this list nor Instinct nor Plumb's replaces a veterinary license. 둘 중 하나가 면허를 대신하지는 않습니다.

Legal home: [Wiley Five-Minute series](https://books.wiley.com/series/blackwells-five-minute-veterinary-series/). Full text is sold by Wiley / VetConsult, not reconstructed here.

## What this resident actually does with the list

1. Treat the volume name as a **domain**, not as a book to pirate.
2. Pull the night-critical hard stops into `er_safety_card.md` and `resident_brief.py` from **public** sources.
3. If a number is required, open Plumb or the hospital protocol. The 2026 Drug Consult is a reminder that formulary lookup is a licensed job.

No third-party book-mirror URLs belong in this repo.
