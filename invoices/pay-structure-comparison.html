Production vs prosal — number check

Dr. Yujin Kim, DVM, PhD · URvet Care · prepared September 23, 2026

What was written in the original thread: Amy: “$250 per hour per diem and whatever production you decided.” Evan: “23 percent.” That is hourly plus 23% of Gross.

What Evan is saying now: “23% of Gross less the $250/hour. You get the higher, or the delta. Difference paid quarterly. No negative accrual. Cremation paid to a third party is out.” That is prosal, not additive production.

The two formulas

NameFormulaWhen you get extra money
A. Production (your invoices) Hourly + (23% × Gross, positives only) Any positive Gross adds pay
B. Prosal (Evan now) max(Hourly, 23% × Gross) — or Hourly each period, then quarterly max(0, 23%Gross − Hourly) Only if 23% × Gross beats the hourly already paid

Other points from the texts

Quarterly production: they can pay the $250/hour on the regular ADP cycle and true-up production later. That is a timing issue, not a formula change.

No negative accrual: if 23% × Gross is under hourly, they do not claw back the hourly. That only matters under prosal.

Cremation / third-party euthanasia: if that is in Kim Gross, take it out of the 23% base. Need the line items from them to adjust the $10,326.32 / $598.64.
