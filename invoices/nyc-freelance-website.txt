What that site does
  Sends your complaint to the hiring party. They have 20 days to answer.
  If they stay silent, DCWP gives you a “did not respond” notice.
  In court that silence is treated as a violation unless they prove otherwise.
  The website does not deposit money. Double damages are a court remedy,
  not a DCWP check. NYC small claims cap is $10,000, so claim $5,950 unpaid.
  Do not claim $11,900 (double) — it will not fit.

Oct 22, 2026 — do not treat DCWP as automatic
  City law § 20-927(3) and State GBS § 1410(3)(c) exclude
  “any person who is a licensed medical professional.”
  They never define the phrase. A DVM is NYSED-licensed
  (Veterinary Medicine). Coverage is uncertain.
  If DCWP says you are excluded, skip FIFA. File Manhattan
  small claims for the $5,950 contract balance.
  That is the stack that wins. The website is optional.
